Conclusion
Diffusion models generate images by gradually removing noise from a noisy representation. Forward diffusion adds noise, while learned reverse diffusion generates or reconstructs images. These models support image editing, restoration, super-resolution, and text-to-image generation, but they must be used responsibly.
