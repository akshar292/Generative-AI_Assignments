Task 5: Difference Between DCGAN, StyleGAN, and CycleGAN
Prompt used: Explain the difference between DCGAN, StyleGAN, and CycleGAN in simple English in 3–4 lines. Give the main purpose of each model.

ChatGPT's response:
- DCGAN (Deep Convolutional GAN): Uses convolutional neural networks to generate images, often learning from datasets such as handwritten digits or faces.
- StyleGAN: Generates highly realistic images and provides control over visual attributes and styles, especially in face generation.
- CycleGAN: Converts images from one visual domain to another without requiring paired training examples, such as converting horse images into zebra-like images.

Conclusion
GANs consist of two neural networks: a generator that creates synthetic data and a discriminator that attempts to distinguish generated data from real data. Through adversarial training, both networks improve over time, enabling applications such as image generation, image translation, and artistic transformations.
